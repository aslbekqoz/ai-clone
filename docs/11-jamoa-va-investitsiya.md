# 11 — Jamoa, tashkiliy tuzilma va investitsiya

## Jamoa rejasi

| Bosqich | Rollar | Vazifa |
|---|---|---|
| **Asoschilar (0-oy)** | CEO / Product · CTO / Full-stack | Mijozlar bilan suhbat, sotuv, mahsulot; arxitektura, AI yadro |
| **MVP (1–6 oy)** | + Full-stack dasturchi · + Growth/SMM | Dashboard, kanallar; blogerlar bilan hamkorlik, kontent |
| **Launch (6–12 oy)** | + Customer success (UZ/RU) · + ML/AI muhandis | Onboarding, qo'llab-quvvatlash; RAG sifati, o'zbek STT/TTS, evals |
| **O'sish (12–24 oy)** | + Dasturchi #2 · + B2B sotuv · + Dizayner · + CS #2 · + DevOps | Korporativ, global hujayra, xavfsizlik |
| **Kengayish (24–36 oy)** | + Marketing lead · mamlakat menejerlari | MDH bozorlari |

**Asoschilar orasidagi kelishuv (birinchi haftada yozma):** ulushlar, rollar, 4 yillik vesting (1 yil cliff), qaror qabul qilish tartibi, asoschi ketsa nima bo'ladi, IP kompaniyaga tegishli.

**ESOP:** kapitalning 10% i xodimlar opsion puli sifatida ajratiladi (kalit xodimlarni jalb qilish uchun — ayniqsa ML muhandis).

**Maslahatchilar kengashi (0.25–1% ulush, 2 yil vesting):**
1. AI/ML mutaxassisi (RAG va o'zbek NLP)
2. Taniqli o'zbek bloger / kreator (taqsimot va ishonch)
3. Yurist (shaxsiy ma'lumotlar, IT Park, xalqaro tuzilma)
4. SaaS / startup asoschisi (fundraising tajribasi)

## Investitsiya strategiyasi

### Raundlar rejasi

| Raund | Qachon | Summa | Taxminiy baholash | Nima uchun | Shart (milestone) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Grant / bepul mablag'** | 0–6 oy | $10–50k | — | MVP | IT Park grantlari, akselerator dasturlari |
| **Pre-seed** | 2026-dekabr – 2027-fevral | $150k | SAFE, $1.5 mln cap (taxm.) | 18 oylik runway | 10 design partner, 5 to'lov, ishlaydigan MVP |
| **Seed** | 2027-Q4 | $1–1.5 mln | $6–10 mln (taxm.) | MDH kengayish, jamoa 15 kishi | $10k+ MRR, oyiga 15%+ o'sish, churn < 5% |

> Baholashlar — mintaqaviy pre-seed/seed bitimlari uchun taxminiy oraliq; haqiqiy shartlar traction va investorga bog'liq.
